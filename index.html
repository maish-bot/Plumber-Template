FAQ

Common questions

Do you charge a call-out fee?

Our £40 call-out covers the first 30 minutes on site, which is enough for most small fixes. If a job needs more, we give you a fixed price before starting.

How quickly can you get to me in an emergency?

We aim to be with you within 60 minutes for emergencies in our area, day or night.

Are you Gas Safe registered and insured?

Yes. We’re Gas Safe registered and carry full public liability insurance. Ask to see our ID card when we arrive.

Do you guarantee your work?

All workmanship is guaranteed for 12 months. New boilers come with the manufacturer’s warranty on top.

How can I pay?

Card, bank transfer or cash. You only pay once the job is done and you’re happy.

Do you work evenings and weekends?

Planned work runs Monday to Saturday. Emergency call-outs are available 24/7, including bank holidays.
