Water coming through right now?

Ring me, then do these three things while I’m on the way. It can save a ceiling.

0121 555 7890

While you wait

  1. Turn the water off at the stopcockUsually under the kitchen sink. Turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. Switch off electrics near the waterIf it’s near sockets or lights, turn them off at the fuse box.
  3. Catch what you canBuckets and towels down, and move anything valuable out of the way.

07FAQ

Questions people ask

Do you charge a call-out fee?

The £40 call-out covers my first 30 minutes on site, which is enough for most small fixes. If a job needs more, I tell you the price before I start.

How fast can you get here in an emergency?

Usually within the hour if you’re in my area, day or night. I’ll give you a time on the phone.

Are you Gas Safe registered and insured?

Yes. Ask to see my Gas Safe ID card when I arrive, it’s the law for anyone working on gas.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes, 12 months on all my workmanship. New boilers also come with the manufacturer’s warranty.

How do I pay?

Card, bank transfer or cash, once the job’s done and you’re happy with it.

Do you work evenings and weekends?

Planned jobs run Monday to Saturday. Emergencies any time, bank holidays included.
